Why I Love Cats

From my experiences in life I can easily say that CATS are one of if not my favorite animal. There are many reasons for this but it mainly started due to the fact that I have had cats as a part of my life for as long as I can remember. For as far back as I can remember my house has always had at least one fuzzy friend around for me to pet and love. This was due to my mom also loving cats and wanting to have her own feline friend around. Their constant presence in my life along with the love and warmth they have given me are something I wish to share with eveyone I can. This site was created from the desire to share the love I have for cats with other and for those who love them just as much to have a place to come together.
